Freshman Projects Class

Design, fabrication, and testing of a custom thermoelectric converter for CU freshamn projects.

Prototype Creation

Initial CAD modeling focused on a compact design and assembly to optimize heat transfer while in a protoype stage.

Concept & CAD Development

I worked as the head of CAD and rapid prototyping, using SolidWorks to generate models and drawings alongside 3D printers to construct prototype housing.

Testing & Calibration

Bench testing using water to confirm voltage outputs and component lifespan. The shown experiments generated enough electricity to charge an iPhone.

Final Presentation

Alongside my group of 5, I presented to a panel of judges about our design, development, and final product. We won Best in our Section and second overall at a similar events two weeks prior.
