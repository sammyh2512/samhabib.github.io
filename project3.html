SolidWorks Final Project

Individual project focused on reverse engineering and modeling a complete skateboard assembly in SolidWorks using precise physical measurements.

Part Development

Each component was physically measured from an existing skateboard and modeled accurately as SolidWorks part files.

Drawing Creation

Parts were converted into detailed mechanical drawings with full dimensioning and manufacturing ready formatting.

Assembly Modeling

All components were assembled into a complete virtual skateboard, including an exploded view for visualization and documentation.

Final Draft

The finished assembly and drawings were evaluated for dimensional accuracy, design clarity, and real-world representation.
