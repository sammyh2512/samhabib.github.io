CU Boulder Formula SAE

Design, fabrication, and testing of a custom flow measurement system for a university Formula SAE racecar.

Concept & CAD Development

Initial CAD modeling focused on compact aerodynamic packaging and mass-optimized geometry to integrate efficiently into the cooling loop subsystem.

Prototype Fabrication

Machined aluminum housing and sensor wiring assembly, enabling preliminary functional testing of the sensor with controlled flow rates.

Testing & Calibration

Bench calibration using water to confirm accurate data response and ensure linear sensor behavior throughout expected operating ranges.
